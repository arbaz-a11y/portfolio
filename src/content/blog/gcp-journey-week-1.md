Week one of my GCP Associate Cloud Engineer prep. The plan: learn each service by **building a small project**, not by watching courses.

## What I set up this week

- A project + billing account with a hard budget alert
- My first IAM service account and scoped roles
- A tiny Compute Engine instance — created by hand in the console, then again with the `gcloud` CLI

## The thing that clicked

IAM roles are everything. Spending ten minutes on least-privilege scoping taught me more than any diagram.

Next up: the same app, but with Terraform.
